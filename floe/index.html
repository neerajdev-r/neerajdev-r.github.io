Introduction

Introduction

FLOE is a smart IoT platform that enables seamless management of connected devices for a diverse user base — from elderly home users to factory operators and administrators. The platform balances simplicity, scalability, and transparency, ensuring all user roles have tailored experiences.

FLOE is a smart IoT platform that enables seamless management of connected devices for a diverse user base — from elderly home users to factory operators and administrators. The platform balances simplicity, scalability, and transparency, ensuring all user roles have tailored experiences.

Defining the Problem

Defining the Problem

Accessibility for Older Users

Many end-users (60+ age group) required designs that were easy to navigate and visually clear.

Integration Complexity

Factory operators needed workflows to manage devices across multiple platforms.

Scalability

The platform had to support a large number of users and devices while maintaining simplicity.

Transparency and Control

Admins required real-time activity logs and user action tracking.

Research & Discovery

Conducted interviews with 15 users, including end-users, admins, and factory operators, to understand their pain points and workflows.

Key insights included

Older users valued simplicity, readability, and visual clarity.

Factory operators needed step-by-step workflows to manage complex integrations.

Admins required role-based dashboards and real-time tracking of user and device actions.
