ATeam Soft Solutions is a global technology provider offering advanced services in AI, LLMs, automation, and enterprise solutions. The Challenge

The original homepage lacked hierarchy and failed to communicate ATeam's real value. It had no consistent design language, visual overload in key areas, and CTAs that didn’t encourage user action.

Trust

The interface didn’t build confidence — testimonials and social proof were weak or buried.

Visual Clarity

Lack of structure and visual hierarchy made content difficult to scan.

No Design System

There was no component system or style consistency across sections.

CTA Ineffectiveness

Key actions (like “Get a Free Quote”) were lost in noisy backgrounds or misaligned placements.
