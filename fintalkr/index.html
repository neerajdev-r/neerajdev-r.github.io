Defining the Problem

Defining the Problem

Defining the Problem

The Challenge

Advisors had to manually upload voice files from their desktops, wait for email transcriptions, and download summaries days later. This process wasn’t scalable or mobile-friendly.

Key Issues Identified

Manual, multi-step recording and upload process

No biometric or 2FA security for sensitive financial content

AI transcription and PDF generation were hidden and underused

Inconsistent support for client- or meeting-type tagging

No real-time feedback or engagement prompts during recording

Research & Discovery

Research & Discovery

Research & Discovery

We interviewed 12 financial advisors and support staff to understand their daily needs.

Key insights included

Speed was essential. Users wanted one-tap recording from the home screen.

AI-generated transcripts needed to feel trustworthy and professional.

Users expected visual feedback while recording.

Compliance teams demanded biometric login and 2FA.

We also benchmarked tools like Otter, Noted, and Rev to assess onboarding, record flow, and security design patterns.

Ideation & Concept Development

Ideation & Concept Development

Ideation & Concept Development

We mapped a streamlined flow

Login → Dashboard → Record → AI Transcription → PDF Download → Client Tagging

We began with brainstorming sessions to define the core features and functionality of Splacebar, focusing on addressing the key pain points identified in our research.

From that, we prioritized:

A clean 3-tab navigation: Home, Quick Record, Settings

Smart bottom sheets for "Add Details" before recording

In-app biometric flows (Face ID + Touch ID)

Sentiment-aware recording summaries

Support for assigning meetings to multiple clients

Alert-based engagement to prevent inactive or forgotten recordings

Wireframing

An empty state with illustrations and a CTA for first-time users

A dynamic waveform-based recorder with timer

Bottom drawer UI for meeting detail input

Conditional alerts like “Are You Still Talking?” and “Are You Sure?”

Filter drawer to search recordings by Client, Type, Date

Visual Design

Color Palette: Muted neutrals + FinTalkr’s signature purple

Typography: Large, clear sans-serif fonts with strong headings

Illustrations: AI-driven 3D micro-illustrations on splash, modal, and alerts

Icons: Custom icon system supporting accessibility and clarity

Status States: Uploading (orange), Completed (green), Action (red)

Challenges & Solutions

Challenges & Solutions

Challenges & Solutions

Challenge

Idle recordings wasting cloud storage

Multiple clients per session

Confusion after app interruption

PDF access after upload

Data security

Solution

Introduced “Still Talking?” prompts during long silences

Added dynamic tag chips and multi-select dropdown

Banner CTA: “Go Back to Recording” when user returns mid-session

In-app notifications and badge alerts guide user to transcript

Biometric login + 2FA + secure upload confirmation
