Introduction

Introduction

Piggyback Sports is a mobile app designed to transform how users learn about sports betting. By blending educational content with gamification and a supportive community, the app makes complex betting concepts accessible to everyone — from novices to seasoned bettors. The goal was to deliver a platform that balances learning with entertainment while promoting responsible gambling.

Piggyback Sports is a mobile app designed to transform how users learn about sports betting. By blending educational content with gamification and a supportive community, the app makes complex betting concepts accessible to everyone — from novices to seasoned bettors. The goal was to deliver a platform that balances learning with entertainment while promoting responsible gambling.

Defining the Problem

Defining the Problem

The Challenge

Sports betting platforms can be overwhelming for newcomers, and many educational apps fail to engage users. Piggyback Sports aimed to simplify complex betting concepts while keeping users motivated and socially connected.

Educational vs Entertainment Balance

Making learning engaging without sacrificing depth

Responsible Gambling Integration

Promoting safe betting practices

Complex Data Visualization

Simplifying analytics into digestible visuals

Multi-Level User Experience

Designing for both beginners and advanced bettors

Key Insights

Learning Barrier

78% of novice users found traditional betting platforms overwhelming and difficult to understand

Community Value

92% of users expressed interest in learning from more experienced bettors in a community setting

User Motivation

Users were 3x more likely to continue learning when incentivized through gamification elements

Visual Preference

Dark theme with vibrant accents was preferred by 72% of users for extended app usage
