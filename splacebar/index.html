Splacebar

An intuitive storage management app connecting Space Seekers with Space Owners

Timeline

Feb - Mar 2024

Category

B2B IoT Platform

Industry

Inventory Management

Role

Lead UI/UX Designer

Introduction

Reimagining how people find and share storage spaces

Splacebar is a revolutionary platform designed to connect Space Seekers (individuals looking for storage solutions) with Space Owners (those with extra storage space to rent out).

With the rising costs of living and limited urban space, many people struggle to find affordable and convenient storage options for their belongings. Meanwhile, others have unused space that could generate income.

Splacebar bridges this gap with a user-centered design that prioritizes trust, transparency, and ease of use.

Defining the Problem

Understanding the key challenges in storage space sharing

The Challenge

Traditional storage solutions are often expensive, inflexible, and impersonal. Meanwhile, peer-to-peer storage sharing lacks proper security, verification, and user-friendly interfaces.

Trust & Security

Users lack confidence in the security and reliability of peer-to-peer storage solutions.

Space Visualization

Difficulty in visualizing storage spaces makes it hard to determine if a space meets specific needs.

Communication

Inefficient communication between Space Seekers and Space Owners leads to misunderstandings and wasted time.

Research & Discovery

Gathering insights to inform our design decisions

User Interviews

We conducted interviews with 20 potential users across two primary personas

Space Seekers

Frequently move between rental properties

Struggle with limited space in urban apartments

Need temporary storage during life transitions

Concerned about security and accessibility

Space Owners

Have underutilized spaces in their homes or properties

Looking for ways to generate passive income

Concerned about the reliability of renters

Want a hassle-free way to manage space rentals

5

Multiple rounds of user testing helped us refine the design, ensuring that Splacebar meets the needs of both Space Seekers and Space Owners.

Challenges & Solutions

Overcoming obstacles in the design process

Inventory Accuracy

Ensuring accurate inventory tracking posed significant technical and UX challenges, as users needed a simple way to catalog items without missing details.

Solution

We developed a guided inventory process with smart categorization, pre-filled suggestions, and photo integration to streamline item documentation while maintaining accuracy.

Space Measurement Standardization

Different users had different ways of measuring and describing spaces, leading to confusion and mismatched expectations.

Solution

We implemented an AR-based measuring tool that standardizes space dimensions and creates visual representations using common objects for scale (e.g., "This space fits approximately 10 medium moving boxes").

Future Improvements

AI-Powered Inventory Management

Future updates will include AI object recognition to automatically catalog items from photos, reducing the time and effort required for inventory creation.

Community Marketplace

We plan to introduce a marketplace feature that allows Space Owners to offer additional services like pickup, delivery, and item maintenance.

Expanded VR Experience

Virtual reality tours of storage spaces will provide an even more immersive way for Space Seekers to evaluate options before committing.

Conclusion

The Splacebar project demonstrates how thoughtful design can transform a practical need into an intuitive, trustworthy experience. By focusing on the core user challenges of trust, visualization, and communication, we created a platform that serves both Space Seekers and Space Owners with equal consideration.

Our blue-themed design language, with its emphasis on clarity, consistency, and calm, reinforces the sense of security that users need when storing their valuable possessions. Every UI element was crafted to build confidence and reduce friction in the storage- sharing process.

As Splacebar continues to evolve, we remain committed to user-centered design principles, ensuring that future features and improvements serve the real needs of our community while maintaining the simplicity and elegance that defines the platform.
